# Asset provenance

- The two Framer screenshots supplied in chat guide the visual design. No source Framer project, downloadable source photos, official brand-logo files or product media were supplied.
- `public/images/hero.webp` is original AI-generated concept artwork produced for this project: an unbranded red sport motorcycle in a dark studio. It is **not** a verified depiction of a particular Ducati, BMW or Yamaha model. The page labels it as concept/demo art. Its use does not establish commercial trademark rights or a real physical product.
- `carbon.svg`, `model.svg` and `part-*.svg` are original SVG placeholder illustrations created in this project. The same generic motorcycle illustration intentionally stands in for models until owner-provided photos are uploaded. These are not product photographs.
- Marque names and the stylized monochrome selector badges are reference placeholders identifying supported fitment brands. Replace them with owner-provided, approved brand assets and review brand-use rights before launch. The footer states that the concept is independent of the manufacturers.
- Icons are supplied by lucide-react (ISC license). System Arial/Helvetica fonts avoid third-party font requests. No stock photography was scraped or represented as licensed commercial imagery.
- Owner uploads are stored in a public bucket. The owner must have rights to publish them. No photographs, certificates, measured weights, performance results, manufacturer affiliations or real stock are invented.
